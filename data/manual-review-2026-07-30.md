**Verdict: keep transformationalist. Basis: evidenced (church's own words).**

**Verdict: keep transformationalist. Basis: evidenced.** Sources to add: theshepherds.church blog
distinctives, The PRODCAST, the Fox News piece.

## Method note

Three automated passes produced three different tallies of the same 104 churches, and each was
wrong in the same direction: confident about absence. A church's public conviction frequently
lives in a podcast, a blog series, a sermon archive or a news story — none of which sit on the
homepage a scraper reads. **Absence of evidence on a homepage is not evidence of absence**, and
for this directory the cost of a false demotion is higher than the cost of leaving a row
unverified a little longer.
