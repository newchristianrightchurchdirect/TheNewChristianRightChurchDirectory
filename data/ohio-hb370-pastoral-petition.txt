# The petition text signed by each man below:
#   "I, the undersigned, wholeheartedly and unequivocally support H.B. 370, the Ohio Prenatal
#    Equal Protection Act, and urge our esteemed and honorable legislature to give passage to it
#    out of obedience to King Jesus."
#
# H.B. 370, the Ohio Prenatal Equal Protection Act, was introduced in the Ohio House in June 2025.
# It defines personhood as commencing at fertilisation and would grant preborn persons the same
# legal protections as born persons — i.e. abolition, not regulation. Backed by End Abortion Ohio,
# "a distinctively Christian organization that is committed to working toward the legal abolition
# of abortion in Ohio."
